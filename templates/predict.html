Readmission Risk Prediction

Decision-support prototype trained on historical (synthetic) data. Scores estimate the chance of readmission within 30 days of discharge; they are not diagnoses and must not replace clinical judgement.

1. Train & compare models

Label: readmitted within 30 days. Features: age, gender, department, diagnosis, length of stay, prior admissions, days since last discharge and discharge vitals. Train/test split is by patient (no one appears in both). Numeric features are imputed and scaled, categories one-hot encoded. Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting (and XGBoost if installed) are compared; the best F1 is saved.

Recall = share of real readmissions caught; precision = share of flagged patients who were really readmitted. For a screening list, recall usually matters more, and models use balanced class weights for that reason.

Interpreting the metrics

With only about {{ bundle.readmit_rate }}% of discharges readmitted, plain accuracy is misleading (a model that always says “no” scores high). F1 balances precision and recall, so it is used to choose the saved model; ROC-AUC shows how well scores rank high-risk above low-risk patients independent of the cut-off.
